# BRIEF — Monolith Web Studio

## Positioning

**Award-level craft for businesses that usually get a template.**

Clubs, venues, cafés, makers, musicians and local brands across South East Queensland normally choose between a cheap template and nothing. Monolith gives them a site that feels like it came from a top international studio, while staying fast, accessible and easy to update.

Key differentiators to communicate:
- One person, start to finish. You talk to the maker, not an account manager.
- Artistic, memorable design **and** strong performance and accessibility scores. Most showpiece sites fail one of those; Monolith's do both.
- Local: based in Wamuran, at the foot of the Glasshouse Mountains.

## Audiences

1. **Primary: local small and medium businesses and organisations** (Moreton Bay, Sunshine Coast, Brisbane). Often not technical. They need to understand the offer, trust it, and see a price range fast.
2. **Secondary: agencies and design-literate buyers** who may subcontract or commission flagship work. They judge the craft, motion and technical quality.
3. **Tertiary: award juries and the design community** (Awwwards, CSS Design Awards, FWA).

The 3D experience serves audiences 2 and 3. The plain, clear layer serves audience 1. Both are always present.

## Voice

Confident, warm, plain-spoken, Australian. Short sentences. No jargon ("synergy", "solutions", "leverage"). First person singular ("I"). Volcanic and stone metaphors are welcome in headlines but must never obscure meaning in body copy.
